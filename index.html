The ten-finger guide

Home row first. Your hands barely move from there.

1. Find the bumps

Feel the raised bars on F and J. Rest your index fingers there without looking. Those two keys anchor every other movement.

2. Let each finger own its keys

Fingers move in straight columns and curl back to home. Reach from the knuckle, not the wrist.

3. Thumbs stay on the space bar

Whichever thumb is closer presses space. Your other fingers never leave home row for it.

About this game

Built as a single page that runs offline. Practice text is original or public-domain material: no song lyrics and no copyrighted text. Music is synthesised live in your browser, so there are no recordings to licence. Backgrounds are original CSS scenes, not affiliated with any group or label.

Defaults: 90% to unlock, text at 34px, key sounds on, music off (turn it on in settings), backdrop "Stage" at 28% dim.

If it feels hard

Slow down until you stop looking at your hands. Accuracy first, speed follows. Turn the timer off in settings for a pressure-free pass, and use Restart as often as you like.

Use Esc to pause and Tab then Enter on a button when you need the mouse-free way.

How progress works

Every keystroke counts. Accuracy is correct presses divided by all presses, including corrections. Reach the unlock threshold and the next level opens.

Score adds for each accurate word and for streaks, and draws back gently for misses. Nothing ever goes below zero.

Paused

Take a breath. Your progress is held.

Save your score

Your name, points, and accuracy will be visible on the public leaderboard. A nickname is fine. Only the top three records are kept.
